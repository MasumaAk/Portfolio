Large-Scale ADRD Outcomes Research

Research collaboration, Binghamton University — Python, GLMs

Analyzed a seven-year national healthcare dataset spanning roughly 22.6 million records, narrowed to a 2.3-million-patient ADRD cohort, to examine mortality, length of stay, and hospital cost. Applied multivariable statistical modeling — logistic, negative-binomial, and gamma regression — to evaluate patient- and hospital-level factors tied to each outcome. One finding looked backwards at first — a documented fall was associated with lower mortality odds — and rather than treating that as a real protective effect, we traced it to a plausible explanation: patients well enough to have a fall recorded, and watched more closely afterward, aren't a random group to begin with.
